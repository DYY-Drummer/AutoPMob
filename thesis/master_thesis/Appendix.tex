\section{Weights of the Retrieval Baselines}
Table~\ref{tab:weights} lists the classical baseline under each of the 12 weight pairs. Recall@K
peaks at $(0.4, 0.6)$ in both settings and changes little for $w_{\mathrm{t}} \le 0.5$ (0.598 to
0.607 in Setting~A), whereas the text term alone reaches only 0.120 and 0.153: the description
alone rarely identifies the equations of a case. BM25 and E5 peak at $(0.1, 0.9)$ and stay at the
level of the best TF-IDF mixture.
