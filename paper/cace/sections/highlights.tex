\item Equation selection for physical model building is posed as equation-set retrieval.
\item A two-stage method reranks candidates by their relation to other equations.
\item Recall@20 rises from \RecABase{} to \RecAProp{} over classical retrieval on \NCases{} cases.
\item The method beats two LLMs that read the whole database of \NEq{} equations.
\item Shared variables, not topical similarity, explain most of the improvement.
